Tool 1

🔍 Keyword Search

Find out how crowded a keyword is before you create a product for it.

  1. Open the "KW Search" tabType a keyword (for example "fall") and press Run.
  2. Read the resultsYou get the keyword, its suggestions and sub-suggestions, each with a results count and a competition label: High, Med or Low.
  3. Spot the gapsKeywords with a Low or Med label and a decent number of results are good candidates for a new product.
  4. Export what you needUse Export CSV, Copy All or Copy Keywords at the bottom of the panel.
Tool 2

🔎 Deep Search KW

Go beyond the obvious keywords and discover long-tail ideas.

  1. Open the "Deep Search KW" tabType your main keyword and press Analyze.
  2. Wait for the two phasesThe tool first collects keyword ideas, then fetches the result count for each one. A progress bar shows how far it is, and you can press Stop at any time.
  3. Review the full listEvery keyword comes with its results count and competition label, so you can sort out the best opportunities quickly.
  4. Download the reportPress "Download CSV Report" to keep the whole list.
Tool 3

📥 Multi-Page Scraper

Study what's already on page 1, 2, 3… of a TpT search, and see which products get the most views.

  1. Search on TpTRun any search on TeachersPayTeachers as usual.
  2. Open the "Multi-Page CSV" tabChoose how many pages to scan, and a minimum number of views if you want to filter.
  3. Press "Start Scraping"The tool collects the products and shows the top results ranked by views, with their publish date.
  4. Download the CSVPress "Download CSV" to analyze competitors in a spreadsheet.
Tool 4

🏷️ Inline badges on TpT results

While you browse TpT search results, small badges appear under each product title.

  1. Search on TpT as usualNo need to open the panel.
  2. Read the badgesUnder each product title you see its publish date and view count, so you can tell at a glance what is new, old, popular or quiet.
