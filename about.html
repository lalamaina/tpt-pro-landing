Hi, I'm a teacher who builds tools

Teaching since 2012, selling on TpT, and creating digital resources and tools for other teacher-sellers.

My story

I have been a teacher since 2012. Years in the classroom taught me what students need and what teachers are short of: time, and materials that actually work.

That is why I started creating digital resources and selling them on TeachersPayTeachers. Running a TpT store is real work. You research niches, watch competitors, plan sales and keep an eye on your numbers.

I found myself repeating the same tedious tasks again and again, so I started building small tools to do them for me. Then I shared them with other sellers. That is how TpT Pro Tools began.
